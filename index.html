About Terry

Terry Mahoney grew up in Western Springs, Illinois, certain he was going to be a professional golfer. He wasn’t. What followed was a life built from the wreckage of abandoned plans: college dropout, Marine codebreaker stationed on Guam, Darden MBA, and — on his first scuba dive off the Phi Phi Islands on December 26, 2004 — survivor of the Indian Ocean tsunami.

He lives in Atlanta with his wife and their four children. This is his first book.
